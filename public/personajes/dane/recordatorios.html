⚠️ Tactical Reminders

  • 🌑 Umbral Sight: In total darkness, you are INVISIBLE to creatures relying on Darkvision (Advantage on attacks, Disadvantage to be hit). 90 ft darkvision range.
  • 💨 Cunning Action: Bonus Action to Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
  • 🗡️ Assassinate: Advantage on attacks against any creature that hasn't taken a turn. Surprised targets take auto-critical hits.
  • 🎯 Piercer: Reroll one low piercing damage die once per turn (must use the new roll).
  • 🏃 Dread Ambusher: +10 ft of movement during your first turn of combat.
