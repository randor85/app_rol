🔥 4. Rasgos de clase y raza

Feat Sharpshooter (Nvl 4)

  • Attacking at long range doesn't impose disadvantage.
  • Attacks ignore half cover and three-quarters cover.
  • Take -5 penalty to attack roll to add +10 to the attack's damage.

Feat Piercer (Nvl 4)

  • Once per turn, you can reroll one of your damage dice that deals piercing damage (you must use the new roll).
  • When you score a critical hit that deals piercing damage to a creature, you can roll one additional damage die when determining the extra piercing damage.

Ranger Deft Explorer: Canny (Nvl 1)

  • Double proficiency bonus for one skill (Perception).
  • Learn two additional languages (Update with DM).

Trasfondo Profession: Blacksmith

Your weapons have a +1 bonus to attack rolls. Using a forge and materials, you can upgrade your party members' weapons to add this same +1 bonus.

Trasfondo Profession: Pilot

You have expertise in piloting any vehicle (airships, sea vessels, land transports). You possess a supernatural intuition for navigation, plotting flawless courses by reading stars and wind currents, ensuring you never lose direction even in treacherous hazards.

Ranger Dread Ambusher

+WIS mod to initiative. +10ft speed on first turn. 1 extra attack on first turn Attack action (+1d8 dmg).

Clase Extra Attack & Cunning Action

Attack twice. Bonus action to Dash, Disengage, or Hide.

Ranger Favored Foe (4/descanso largo)

Cuando impactás a una criatura con un ataque, podés marcarla como enemigo predilecto durante 1 minuto (sin gastar acción, como concentración). La primera vez en cada uno de tus turnos que le pegás a ese enemigo, le hacés +1d4 de daño extra.

Ranger Primeval Awareness

Acción + 1 espacio de conjuro para detectar, dentro de 1 milla (6 en terreno favorito), si hay aberraciones, celestiales, dragones, elementales, feéricos, infernales o no-muertos en la zona, durante 1 minuto por nivel del espacio gastado.

Racial Hellish Resistance

Resistencia al daño de fuego.

Racial Fly Speed 30ft

Alas tipo murciélago; volás a 30 ft mientras no lleves armadura pesada. Reemplaza el rasgo racial Infernal Legacy (Dane no tiene Thaumaturgy/Hellish Rebuke/Darkness raciales).

⚠️ Tactical Reminders

  • 🌑 Umbral Sight: In total darkness, you are INVISIBLE to creatures relying on Darkvision (Advantage on attacks, Disadvantage to be hit). 90 ft darkvision range.
  • 💨 Cunning Action: Bonus Action to Dash, Disengage, or Hide.
  • 🗡️ Assassinate: Advantage on attacks against any creature that hasn't taken a turn. Surprised targets take auto-critical hits.
  • 🎯 Piercer: Reroll one low piercing damage die once per turn (must use the new roll).
  • 🏃 Dread Ambusher: +10 ft of movement during your first turn of combat.
