Feats & Optional Features

Sharpshooter (Ranger Level 4)

  • Attacking at long range doesn't impose disadvantage.
  • Attacks ignore half cover and three-quarters cover.
  • Take -5 penalty to attack roll to add +10 to the attack's damage.

Piercer (Rogue Level 4)

  • Once per turn, you can reroll one of your damage dice that deals piercing damage (you must use the new roll).
  • When you score a critical hit that deals piercing damage to a creature, you can roll one additional damage die when determining the extra piercing damage.

Deft Explorer: Canny (Ranger Level 1)

  • Double proficiency bonus for one skill (Perception).
  • Learn two additional languages (Update with DM).

Features & Traits

Profession (Blacksmith): Your weapons have a +1 bonus to attack rolls. Using a forge and materials, you can upgrade your party members' weapons to add this same +1 bonus.

Profession (Pilot): You have expertise in piloting any vehicle (airships, sea vessels, land transports). You possess a supernatural intuition for navigation, plotting flawless courses by reading stars and wind currents, ensuring you never lose direction even in treacherous hazards.

Dread Ambusher: +WIS mod to initiative. +10ft speed on first turn. 1 extra attack on first turn Attack action (+1d8 dmg).

Extra Attack & Cunning Action: Attack twice. Bonus action to Dash, Disengage, or Hide.

  • 🌑 Umbral Sight: In total darkness, you are INVISIBLE to creatures relying on Darkvision (Advantage on attacks, Disadvantage to be hit). 90 ft darkvision range.
